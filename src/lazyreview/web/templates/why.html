What is this?

lazyreview is a local tool for managing agentic pull-request reviews. It keeps track of the PRs waiting on you, kicks off review runs with your preferred agent and model, stores the findings, and helps you consolidate feedback before anything hits GitHub. The work stays on your machine — clones, logs, and history included — so you can review at your own pace without babysitting a chat session.

Why “lazyreview”?

I’m a fan of lazygit, lazyvim, and lazydocker — tools that take a tedious everyday workflow and make it feel light without stripping away power. This project is a nod to that family: same spirit, applied to code review with AI agents. Less ceremony in the loop, more time actually reading the diff.
